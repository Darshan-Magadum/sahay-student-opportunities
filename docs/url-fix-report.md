# Sahay — Scholarship Application URL Audit & Correction Report

## 1. Executive Summary

During the audit of the live scholarship ingestion pipeline and MongoDB records, an issue was identified where the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) ingestion adapter previously set `applicationUrl` to the scheme guideline PDF (`guidelinesUrl`), rather than an official student application portal. As a result, clicking "Apply" for several scholarships opened PDF notices instead of official web submission pages. Furthermore, some records had relative URLs (e.g., `/public/schemeGuidelines/DEPDGuidelines_1.pdf`) or generic homepage links.

### Corrections Applied:
1. **Separation of Concerns in Data Schema**:
   - `applicationUrl`: Guaranteed HTTPS application or registration portal (never a PDF).
   - `officialSchemeUrl`: Official scheme or ministry informational portal (e.g. AICTE student development schemes, UGC, Ministry of Tribal Affairs).
   - `guidelinesUrl`: Official guideline or notice document (PDF), normalized to absolute HTTPS.
   - `hasDirectApply`: Boolean flag indicating whether student online application is open on a central/dedicated portal.
   - `sourceUrl`: Preserved original source URL (`https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships`).

2. **Destination Hierarchy**:
   - **Dedicated Scheme Portals**: Identified and mapped schemes with independent application portals (e.g., AICTE-JK PMSSS for J&K/Ladakh, Ministry of Tribal Affairs Fellowship portal, Free Coaching for SCs/OBCs).
   - **Central NSP One-Time Registration (OTR) Portal**: Mapped central and AICTE/UGC schemes submitted through the official government portal (`https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/login-page`).
   - **Schemes Without Direct Online Submission**: Schemes administered via institutional nomination (e.g. National Renewable Energy Fellowship) have `applicationUrl: null` and `hasDirectApply: false`, with official ministry link `https://mnre.gov.in/` labeled "Visit official scholarship website".
   - **UI Safeguard**: The Apply button never treats a `.pdf` link as an application destination. PDF notices are exclusively displayed as supporting documents under "View Official Guidelines / Notice (PDF)" when explicitly requested.
   - **Internship Flow**: AICTE internship application URLs (`https://internship.aicte-india.org/internships/<ID>`) were verified and preserved intact.
